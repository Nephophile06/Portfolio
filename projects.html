Sketchify -> A Full-Stack E-Commerce Platform

4:30 min Walkthrough GitHub Repo

“The vision is to create a space that reduces clutter and brings visual comfort in browsing to creative minds.”

A full-featured, pastel-themed e-commerce web platform crafted for stationery and art supplies. Features dynamic category browsing, interactive AJAX cart with bounce alerts, dual payment gateways (Stripe & SSLCommerz for bKash/Nagad), automated A4 invoice generation with email delivery, and an admin analytics dashboard with CSV exports.

Email Invoices & 8-Digit Backup Auth
Admin Analytics & CSV Reports
